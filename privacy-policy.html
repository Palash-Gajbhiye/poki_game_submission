Privacy Policy

Werewolf Classic — Multiplayer Browser Game

Last Updated: July 21, 2026

1 Introduction

Werewolf Classic is a free multiplayer browser game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy covers only data handled by the game itself.

2 Data We Collect and Why

While guest accounts require no login or personal data, the game supports authentication via Google Sign-In to offer profile persistence (usernames, avatars, settings). When you log in, we collect and store the following data in our database:

  • Unique Identifier (ID): A platform-specific user ID to associate your profile.
  • Username & Avatar URL: To display your chosen nickname and avatar image to other players in multiplayer rooms.
  • Device Fingerprint / Ban Status: To enforce room safety rules and prevent ban-bypass.

Any temporary guest preferences are stored locally in your browser only using cookies or Local Storage and are never sent to our servers.

3 External Services & Subprocessors

To provide real-time matchmaking, multiplayer gameplay, and persistent user profiles, this game connects to the following external services:

🔥 Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud)

Used to store persistent profiles for logged-in users (usernames, avatars, ban states) and matchmaking room metadata. Matchmaking data (active room codes, participant counts) is ephemeral and automatically deleted when rooms close.

🔗 PeerJS Signaling Server (P2P Connectivity)

A signaling server facilitates direct WebRTC peer-to-peer connections between players. Only temporary session connection IDs pass through this server. Actual game commands and in-game chat are sent directly between players' browsers and are never processed or logged by our servers.

🎮 Google Identity Services

Used for optional user authentication. Google may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or use cookies. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

4 In-Game Chat

Players may communicate via in-game text chat. All messages are transmitted directly Peer-to-Peer — they are never stored or logged on any server. Every message passes through a local, offline profanity filter before being sent, in line with platform safety guidelines.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

This game may save your chosen username, user ID, or platform authentication preferences locally using your browser's local storage or cookies (e.g., savedgame_werewolf_username). This data is not used for tracking or advertising outside the scope of game features. You may clear it at any time via your browser settings.

6 Contact

For privacy concerns related to this game's data practices, please contact the developer via the game's official repository or support channels.
