Privacy Policy

Werewolf Classic — Multiplayer Browser Game on Poki

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

1 Introduction

Werewolf Classic is a free multiplayer browser game available on Poki. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Poki's platform manages advertising and core platform privacy compliance — this policy covers only data handled by the game itself. Please also refer to Poki's Privacy Policy at poki.com for full platform-wide details.

2 No Personal Data Collected

This game does not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). There is no account system, no login, and no tracking built into the game.

Any temporary preferences (such as your chosen username) are stored locally in your browser only and are never sent to our servers.

3 External Services (Approved by Poki)

To provide real-time multiplayer functionality, this game connects to the following external services, approved by Poki as part of the game's publishing process:

🔥 Firebase Realtime Database (Google)

Used for matchmaking and public room discovery. When you create a public room, the room name, username, and player count are temporarily stored. This data is ephemeral — automatically deleted when the room closes or the host disconnects. No personal data is stored and no permanent logs are kept.

🔗 PeerJS Signaling Server (Custom / Self-Hosted)

A dedicated PeerJS server facilitates WebRTC peer-to-peer connections between players. Only temporary connection IDs pass through this server. All actual game data (moves, chat) is transmitted directly between players' browsers and never touches our server.

4 In-Game Chat

Players may communicate via in-game text chat. All messages are transmitted directly Peer-to-Peer — they are never stored or logged on any server. Every message passes through a local, offline profanity filter before being sent, in line with Poki's community safety standards.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

This game may save your chosen username locally using your browser's local storage or cookies (e.g., savedgame_werewolf_username). This data never leaves your device and is not used for tracking or advertising. You may clear it at any time via your browser settings.

6 Contact

For privacy concerns related to this game's data practices, please contact the developer via the game's official repository or support channels. For platform-wide privacy concerns (advertising, etc.), please visit poki.com.
