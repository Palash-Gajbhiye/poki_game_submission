📖 How to Play

🎯 Objective

Werewolf is a social deduction game. Innocents must find and eliminate the Werewolves before they are outnumbered. Werewolves must secretly kill villagers until they equal or outnumber the innocents.

⚡ Quick Play vs 🎮 Custom Games

  • Quick Play — Instantly places you in an available public room. No setup required — just jump in and play!
  • Custom Games — Browse public rooms, create your own room with custom settings, or join a private room using a Room Code shared by the host.

👥 Roles

  • 🧑 Villager — No special ability. Discuss and vote wisely.
  • 🐺 Werewolf — Secretly kills one player each night. If there are multiple Werewolves, you can see who your teammates are.
  • 💉 Doctor — Saves one player from death each night. You can save yourself!
  • 🔍 Detective — Investigates one player per night. You will learn if that player is a Werewolf or not — use this information wisely.

🌙 Night Phase

All players act secretly at the same time. Werewolves vote on who to kill. The Doctor picks someone to protect. The Detective investigates one player. Actions are resolved and revealed at the start of Day.

☀️ Day Phase

The village learns who (if anyone) was killed overnight. All players discuss openly — share suspicions, alibis, and Detective findings. Dead players become Spectators and can watch but not speak.

🗳️ Voting Phase

Players vote to eliminate a suspect. The player with the most votes is eliminated and their role is revealed to all. In case of a tie, no one is eliminated.

🏆 Winning

Innocent Team wins when all Werewolves are eliminated.
Evil Team wins when Werewolves equal or outnumber the innocents.

👑 Host Controls

  • The room creator is the Host.
  • The Host can kick players from the lobby before the game starts.
  • The Host can set the room to Public or Private. Private rooms require a Room Code to join.
  • The Host starts the game when enough players have joined.

💡 Tips

  • Pay attention to who accuses whom — Werewolves often try to redirect suspicion.
  • The Detective should share clues carefully to avoid being targeted at night.
  • The Doctor should try to protect the Detective if they suspect one is in the game.
  • Werewolves should blend in, act calm, and avoid being too quiet or too aggressive.
